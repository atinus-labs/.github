**Definition.** *Atinus* is a collective that solves technical problems and builds open-source software.

**Axiom I.** Understand the problem before you write the code.<br />
**Axiom II.** Prefer the simple solution.<br />
**Axiom III.** Build in the open.

**Conjecture.** Every hard problem has an elegant solution.<br />
*Proof.* In progress.

### Correspondence

The Greek in our header is the line said to have been carved above the door of Plato's Academy: *“Let no one ignorant of geometry enter.”* Our door is open.

Bring a problem, an idea, or a fix. You don't need to be on the team to contribute.
